  1. Undergraduate Research Fellow (UROP)

    DICE Lab, Royal Holloway, with Prof. Kostas Stathis

    Jun – Sep 2026 Funded

    • The only first-year selected for the funded UROP. 30+ applied. First author of the resulting report.
    • Found that model tier sets the ceiling on how far an LLM can abstract. Scaffolding decides whether it reaches that ceiling.
    • Causal experiments varied grid size (N = 5, 8, 11). Unguided models memorise example sizes. They do not learn general Prolog rules.
    • The scaffolding effect: a schema in the prompt lifted pass rates from 0% to 100% on all 6 frontier models.
    • Built an open-source pipeline: 10 models, 6 frontier APIs and 4 local via Ollama. 40 configurations × 20 samples per cell. Automated Prolog repair loop.
    • Three-layer scoring: syntax, runtime, semantic F1. Found and fixed a silent evaluator bug. It zeroed every rule-based score.
